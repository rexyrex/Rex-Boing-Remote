Ask an AI agent about your servers, keep up to 30 days of history on them, and a rex that starts green.

**Ask an AI agent**
- Rex Boing Remote is now an MCP server. Add it to Claude Code, Codex or any other MCP client (Settings › General › AI agents has the commands, with this copy's path) and ask which servers are up, what they read now, what was busiest, and what happened over the last 48 hours.
- Five read-only tools: `list_servers`, `get_server_snapshot`, `get_server_processes`, `get_server_history` and `get_server_process_history`. Name a server by its name or its id.
- Nothing new is asked of your servers, and there is no network listener: the agent runs the app's own executable, which asks the running app over a message port local to your login session.
- A call opens the app quietly in the menu bar when it isn't running. **Let AI agents read your servers' readings** in Settings › General turns it all off.

**History on the server**
- A server that keeps history while this Mac is away can now keep 7, 14 or 30 days instead of 48 hours: **Keep on the server** in Settings › Servers. Choosing one updates an agent too old to know it.
- Every quarter hour the server also records what each process did over the whole of it: mean and peak CPU, peak memory, disk reads and writes, and GPU.
- `rex-boing-agent history` on the server prints what it kept over any range (the vitals, the processes that used the most, the busiest quarter hours), or JSON with `--json`.
- The app is still sent 48 hours of vitals and 24 of process tables, so a long retention costs disk on the server only.

**The menu bar**
- A live server's rex now rests in green, then turns orange past 70% CPU and red at 100%. An offline server's rex stays dimmed as before.
- Prefer the old look? **Character colour** in Settings › General switches to White → Red, which follows the menu bar: black on a light one.

**Updates**
- Once a day the app asks GitHub for its latest release, and when a newer one is out says so over the fleet list, in the window, at the top of the right-click menu and in Settings, with a link to the release. It downloads and installs nothing itself, the request carries nothing about this Mac or your servers, and **Check for new versions** in Settings › General turns it off.

To get the history changes on a server already set up, press **Update Agent** in Settings › Servers (or **Repair…**, for a server reached through your own SSH setup).
